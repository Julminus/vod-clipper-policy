vod-clipper privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

vod-clipper is a personal desktop application. It runs only on its owner's own Windows computer and is used only by its owner. It turns long videos (the owner's own streams, and videos whose creators have given the owner permission) into short vertical clips, and uploads them to YouTube channels the owner has connected to it. It is not a public service: it has no website login, no accounts and no other users.

Google user data it accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, vod-clipper asks Google for two permissions (scopes):

  • youtube.upload ("Manage your YouTube videos"): to upload each clip the owner has approved to the connected channel, with its title, description, tags and privacy setting (YouTube Data API videos.insert).
  • youtube.readonly ("View your YouTube account"): to read the connected channel's ID, name and handle, so the right channel is shown and chosen (channels.list), and to check each uploaded clip afterwards: whether YouTube finished processing it and what its privacy setting is (videos.list).

vod-clipper does not read, change or delete any other video, playlist, comment, subscription or account information.

How the data is used and where it is kept

  • The Google sign-in for each connected channel (an OAuth refresh token) is stored only on the owner's computer, in Windows Credential Manager.
  • Each connected channel's ID, name and handle, and for each uploaded clip its YouTube video ID, link, processing status and privacy setting, are kept in a database on the owner's computer, so the owner can see what was uploaded and whether it finished.
  • All video processing, including transcription and the AI that picks clips, runs locally on the owner's computer. No video and no Google user data is sent to any AI service or other third party.

Sharing

vod-clipper sends Google user data to no server other than Google's own APIs. It does not sell, share or transfer that data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

vod-clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. vod-clipper uses YouTube API Services: by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

Keeping and deleting data

  • A channel's sign-in is kept until the channel is disconnected. Disconnecting it on the dashboard's Channels page, or with python -m clipper youtube-disconnect, deletes the stored sign-in from the computer at once.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time from the Google account, at myaccount.google.com/permissions. vod-clipper can then no longer reach the channel.
  • The upload records (video IDs, links, statuses) stay in the local database as the owner's record of what was published, and are deleted with it. Downloaded videos and rendered clips are deleted from the computer automatically once their clips have been uploaded and confirmed, or rejected.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub, or use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
